# V4 Creative Brief — Issue 1: "The Idea That Started Everything"

## Vision
Images teach. Text supports. The reader should understand the Turing Machine from the PICTURES ALONE — even if they skipped every narration panel.

## Key Differentiators from V3
1. **Concepts delivered visually** — image sequences that teach, not just illustrate
2. **More panels per page** — graphic novel density (6-10 panels per page, ~70 images total)
3. **Scribble as active demonstrator** — not commenting on text, but performing/showing concepts
4. **The meta-moment** — Scribble discovers it IS a Turing Machine (Page 4 climax)
5. **Cinematic variety** — wide establishing shots, intimate close-ups, dramatic angles
6. **Concept-teaching sequences** — multi-panel visual explanations replacing text blocks

## The Meta-Moment (Page 4 Climax)
Scribble was designed as a Turing Machine embodiment:
- Magnifying glass = READ HEAD (examines symbols)
- Pencil = WRITE HEAD (writes symbols)
- Paper tape scarf = THE TAPE (infinite strip of symbols)
- Onewheel = movement along the tape

On Page 4, after explaining the Turing Machine's three components, Scribble looks at its own hands, its own tape scarf, and has the realization: "Wait... am I...?" This is the emotional and intellectual centerpiece of the entire issue.
