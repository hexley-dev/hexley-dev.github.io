## REAL PROJECTS

**1. Climate TRACE** — climatetrace.org
A coalition of 100+ universities and AI researchers tracks 660 million emission sources worldwide using satellite heat signatures and machine learning. Monthly data, 60-day lag. Not government estimates — actual measured pollution from actual smokestacks, ships, and farms. San Francisco used it to find one ferry route switch saves 2,000 tons of CO₂/year. Maersk uses it to identify which of their ships to retire first. *It made the invisible visible.*

**2. Open Climate Fix** — openclimatefix.org
A London nonprofit built an open-source AI that forecasts solar power output for the UK's National Grid. Result: 40% more accurate solar forecasts, 300,000 tons of CO₂ saved annually, £30M/year in reserve capacity freed. In 2024 they released **Quartz Solar OS** — a free dashboard any homeowner with solar panels can use to see exactly how much power their roof will generate, hour by hour. Built with Google DeepMind. Free. Open source.

**3. Google Tapestry / PJM Grid AI** — energy.gov
Google's "Tapestry" AI partnership with PJM (the largest U.S. grid operator, serving 65 million people) uses machine learning to cut the backlog of new solar and wind projects waiting for grid connection approval — from years down to months. The AI ingests grid topology maps, historical congestion, and equipment ratings, then simulates thousands of "what if a solar farm connects here?" scenarios in seconds. Thousands of renewable projects are sitting in queue. This unsticks them.

**4. MORFO Forest Monitoring AI** — morfo.rest
Drones + AI that can detect individual seedlings 6 months after planting, ID species, and flag failures early. Used by reforestation organizations across Latin America and Africa. Before this tool, you planted trees and hoped. Now you know which ones are dying and why, while there's still time to intervene.

**5. Iron Horse Vineyards Sensor Network** — California wine country
A real working vineyard (not a tech demo) with field sensors measuring soil moisture, temperature, fog, and CO₂, plus drones with multispectral cameras sweeping over vines to detect plant stress invisible to the eye. The farmer sees plant health in real time and waters precisely — cutting water use while increasing yield. Model for what small-scale precision farming looks like in practice.

## YOUR MOVE

**Learn climate data science:** Climate Change AI (climatechange.ai) has free courses, papers, and tutorials. A student opens a Jupyter notebook, loads a public emissions dataset, and trains a simple model that predicts next month's power plant output from weather data. It's three hours of work. It becomes a portfolio project that lands an interview.

**Build with open APIs:** Electricity Maps (electricitymaps.com) and Open Climate Fix both have free APIs. The visual: a laptop, a terminal, a JSON response — and then a small web app appears showing a live carbon intensity meter for your city's grid. Real data. Yours to use.
