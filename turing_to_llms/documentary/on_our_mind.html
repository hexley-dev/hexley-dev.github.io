On Our Mind

Open questions, unresolved explorations, and ideas we're chewing on. This page updates as we work.

  • ☐
    Issue 11 — The Next Chapter Tell one complete story set in the future. Not a gallery. Not a concept page. A real comic issue — 10 pages, same format as Issues 1-10, but looking forward instead of back. One specific breakthrough, one specific person, one specific transformation. We've spent days building concept art pipelines that produce hundreds of images. What we've never done is sit down and write one coherent visual narrative with the same craft the Writer agent gave the original series. The pipeline isn't the problem — we've been generating when we should be storytelling.
  • ☐
    Issue 11 — Story Candidate "From Sequence to Cure" — AlphaFold to a village clinic. The biology story. AlphaFold predicts a protein structure that enables a drug design that reaches a clinic in a place where no pharmaceutical company would have invested. Visual spectacle: protein ribbons folding into shape, a drug molecule clicking into a binding pocket. Human story: a specific person getting treatment that wouldn't have existed five years ago. The technology is invisible in the final beat — it's in the ordinary joy of being well. Grounded in real science: AlphaFold (200M+ structures), Isomorphic Labs, Insilico Medicine ($2.6M from target to trial in 18 months).
  • ☐
    Issue 11 — Story Candidate "The Grid That Learned to Breathe" — technology that restores. The sustainability story. A coal town where the plant closed and the future looked empty. Then: AI-optimized renewables turn the town from energy consumer to energy exporter. The former coal plant engineer now manages a smart grid that reads weather, balances solar and wind, and sells surplus to neighboring counties. The visual spectacle: a weather prediction model resolving into a grid optimization map — swirling atmospheric data becoming clean power allocation. The final beat: the town square on a summer evening, kids playing, the grid humming invisibly, the smokestacks gone. Grounded in real science: DeepMind wind energy (+20% output), Open Climate Fix solar forecasting, Climate TRACE emissions monitoring, Electricity Maps.

    Narrative Issues 9 + 10 should inspire, not just explain. The series shouldn't end at "here's where we are today." It should paint vivid, realistic-optimist futures worth building — and hand the reader the torch.
  • ☑
    Research Prompt length limits — empirically tested. An AI review agent claimed prompts degrade past 400 characters. We accepted this without verification, compressed 240 prompts, and regenerated them — finding no improvement. The actual limit (512 tokens, documented in the diffusers API) was a 5-minute search away. New elements work at every position within the window; only contradictions of earlier tokens fail.
